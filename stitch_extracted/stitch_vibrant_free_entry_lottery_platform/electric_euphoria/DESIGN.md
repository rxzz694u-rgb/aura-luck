## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for an adrenaline-fueled, high-stakes digital playground where anticipation meets modern luxury. The visual tone bridges the viral cultural vitality of Spotify Wrapped, the razor-sharp precision of Tier-1 fintech, and the hypnotic radiance of modern arcade/cyberpunk interfaces. 

It exists to dismantle the stale, transactional aesthetic of legacy state lotteries, replacing it with an electric ritual that feels celebratory, transparent, and effortlessly sleek. 

Key principles driving this system:
- **Luminosity on Obsidian:** High-chroma accents emerge violently and beautifully from deep abyss-like foundations, generating natural visual focal points and urgency without visual clutter.
- **Precision Playfulness:** While colors are dynamic and saturated, layout metrics, typography alignment, and container relationships adhere to strict institutional-grade mathematical scaling.
- **Kinetic Anticipation:** Interactive surfaces respond with tangible atmospheric glows, high-tactility states, and celebratory kinetic feedback.

## Colors

The foundation is built on deep cosmic obsidian tones (`#080811` and `#0D0D1A`), allowing high-voltage neon spectra to cut through with maximum contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primaries (Electric Pulse):** Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) and Hot Magenta (`#EC4899`) form the signature linear and radial gradients driving primary conversions, hero prize indicators, and high-energy touchpoints.
- **Secondaries (Cool Hyperdrive):** Radiant Cyan (`#06B6D4`) and Electric Cobalt (`#3B82F6`) provide a balancing counter-glow for auxiliary prizes, timer telemetry, and user level metrics.
- **Accents (Hyper Accents):** Neon Lime (`#10B981` / `#84CC16`) is strictly reserved for confirmation states, ticket claims, and affirmative feedback. Solar Flare (`#FF5E3A` to `#F59E0B`) fuels critical time expirations, mega-jackpot tiers, and scarcity badges.
- **Surfaces & Overlays:** Background tiers transition through layered depth: Canvas Abyss (`#080811`), Sub-surface (`#0D0D1A`), Container Glass (`#121324`), and Floating Element Slate (`#18192E`). Surfaces leverage translucent alphas ranging from `85%` down to `40%` with layered edge lighting.

## Typography

The type system blends structural geometric display typography with hyper-legible mechanical grotesque forms. 

- **Syne (Display & Numerals):** Deployed for maximum visual punch, dramatic jackpot figures, countdown clocks, and card headlines. Its wide stances and avant-garde curves deliver swagger and visual rhythm. Numbers in countdowns and financial amounts are set with tabular figures (`tnum`) where appropriate to prevent layout jittering.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface & Body):** Serves operational clarity. From micro badge labels to ticket terms, its rounded humanist geometry softens the edge of the futuristic theme while remaining impeccably readable against dark slate cards.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout architecture built on a 4-column phone layout, expanding to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop maxing out at `1280px` canvas width.

- **Mobile Viewports (< 640px):** Single-column stacked cards with safe-area padding prioritized at the viewport bottom to accommodate thumb-driven claim buttons and navigational tabs. Strict 16px lateral padding prevents edge crowding.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** Reflows into a staggered 2-column masonry or 2-column structured grid. Gutters expand to 20px.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Fixed-width central feed (`840px` core action stream or full `1200px` multi-draw dash) flanked by live win-tickers and countdown feeds. 
- **Spacing Scale Rhythm:** The system leverages base-4 spatial metrics. Component interior padding tightly hugs content (`8px` to `16px`), while section transitions preserve dramatic pauses (`32px` to `48px`) to allow luminous cards to breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent dark glass, specular border highlights, and chromatic back-glows rather than traditional muddy drop shadows.

- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#080811`. Ground layer, non-interactive.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Glass):** Solid or 90% opacity `#0D0D1A` with a 1px border colored `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Subtle ambient blur (`backdrop-blur-md`).
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Active Cards):** `#121324` at 80% opacity, bordered by dynamic gradient strokes (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35) 0%, rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.1) 100%)`). Shadows use an ambient, chromatic aura: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Sticky Floating Drawers):** Deep Obsidian Navy `#18192E` at 95% opacity with high surface blur (`backdrop-blur-xl`), an inset 1px highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and expansive drop dispersion `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
- **Active Radiant Glow:** Interactive items gain an outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 2px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a high-radius curved aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`). Soft, friendly contours balance the aggressive, dark neon palette.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Action Buttons):** Completely pill-shaped (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) delivering immediate tactile affordance and thumb-friendly ergonomics.
- **Giveaway Cards & Visual Modules:** Scaled generously to `24px` (`rounded-2xl`) and `32px` (`rounded-3xl`). 
- **Modals & Drawers:** `32px` top-rounded corners on mobile bottom-sheets; uniform `28px` corners on desktop dialogs.
- **Pill Badges:** Infinite border radius encapsulating status counters, ticket counts, and category tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glow Action (Mega CTA):** Full-bleed pill button featuring a 45-degree gradient from Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) to Hot Magenta (`#EC4899`). Type set in `label-lg`, uppercase, extra-bold. Accompanied by a perpetual soft neon glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.35)`), expanding on hover/active states with a spring scaling transition (`scale: 1.02`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Deep navy slate interior (`#121324`), 1px translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text rendered in vibrant cyan (`#06B6D4`). On hover, the border lights up with pure cyan.
- **Free Claim / Instant Action Button:** Saturated Neon Lime (`#10B981`) background with dark obsidian typography (`#080811`). Instant punch for zero-cost participation triggers.

### Cards (Giveaway & Sweepstakes)
- Constructed with Tier 2 surface glass (`#121324` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-blur-md`).
- Multi-layered layout: Hero reward imagery floating in the top hemisphere, backed by a localized radial color bleed matching the prize color profile (e.g., gold items bleed Solar Orange; cash items bleed Neon Lime).
- Bottom hemisphere houses live countdown blocks, ticket accumulation gauges, and the primary entry button. 
- A persistent `1px` gradient stroke surrounds the perimeter with higher light concentration along the top-left edge.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Live Status Badge:** Pill shape with background `rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.15)` and border `1px solid rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.4)`. Contains a pulsing emerald or magenta radio dot (a solid 6px dot surrounded by a radiating CSS ripple) followed by `label-sm` tracking.
- **Odds & Tier Badges:** Dark glass pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with bright colored text indicating multiplier values (e.g., "10X ENTRIES" in `#F59E0B`).

### Countdown Timers & Numeric Displays
- Modular segmented blocks or continuous monospace displays using `numeral-counter` in `Syne`.
- Encased in micro-slate capsules (`#0D0D1A`) with subtle neon top-lines to simulate digital physical machinery.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Pill-shaped or `rounded-xl` containers filled with deep abyss `#0D0D1A`.
- 1px border default `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Focus states smoothly shift to Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)` ring.
- Placeholder text in subdued cool slate (`#64748B`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio / Selection Pods:** Custom circular nodes styled as mini-orbs. Inactive: `#121324` with `1px` border. Active: glowing Hot Magenta (`#EC4899`) center core enclosed by an Electric Blue outer perimeter ring.
- **Checkboxes:** `rounded-md` (6px), transitioning to Neon Lime on check, using an obsidian-colored checkmark SVG.

### Lists & Activity Feeds (Live Winners)
- Minimalist horizontal glass tickers. 
- Avatar circular ringed with an electric gradient glow, accompanied by title, prize amount in bright white (`#FFFFFF`), and time-elapsed tag in muted slate. Separated by `1px` hairline boundaries of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
